02Legal basements

Finished is not the same as legal.

Five things an inspector checks. Miss one and it can’t be insured, can’t be legally rented, and doesn’t count toward your mortgage approval.

  • 01
    Ceiling height of 1.95 mMeasured to the lowest duct, not to the drywall.
  • 02
    Its own entranceSide or rear, lockable, not through the main house.
  • 03
    An egress windowOpenable, large enough to climb out of, reachable from the floor.
  • 04
    Fire separation and linked alarmsBetween the units, and in every sleeping area.
  • 05
    Registered with the CityThe step almost every seller quietly skips.

07FAQ

Good questions.

What areas do you serve?+–

Mississauga first — Cooksville, Malton, Applewood, Meadowvale, Streetsville and Erin Mills — plus the wider GTA including Brampton, Etobicoke, Oakville, Milton and Caledon.

How do I know if a basement is actually legal?+–

Five things: ceiling height, separate entrance, egress window, fire separation, and City registration. I check the permit file before you ever see the listing.

What does it cost to work with you as a buyer?+–

Nothing upfront — my fee is paid through the transaction, standard for Ontario buyer representation.

Can you help with investment properties?+–

It's most of what I do. I'll walk through suite income, financing, and what a property actually cash-flows at before you commit.
